# RecruitedAI Quick-Start Guide

Welcome! This guide walks you through your first session with RecruitedAI, from creating
an account to screening your first CV and running your first candidate–job match. It's
written for recruiters and agency owners — no technical knowledge needed.

Each step matches a real screen in the product. Where something is still being built or
behaves in a way that might surprise you, we say so plainly so you're never left guessing.

---

## Before you begin (30 seconds)

- **Use Chrome or Microsoft Edge.** Everything works in any modern browser, but the
  voice-to-text features (dictating a job description) only work in Chrome or Edge.
- **Have two things handy:** a sample CV (PDF or Word document) and a job description you
  can copy and paste. You'll use both in the first 10 minutes.

---

## Your first 10 minutes — the short version

1. Create your account and land on your dashboard.
2. Screen a CV — upload it and let the AI extract the candidate's profile.
3. Run a match — paste a job description and get a skills-based fit score.
4. Post a vacancy.
5. Add a client.

The dashboard shows a **5-step onboarding checklist** that mirrors these actions and ticks
items off as you complete them. The steps below expand on each.

## Step 1 — Create your account

1. Go to the sign-up page and choose the **"Company / Recruiter"** account type (this is
   selected by default).
2. Enter your **Company Name**, your **first and last name**, your **email**, and a
   **password** (at least 6 characters).
3. Pick your **preferred currency** (defaults to **ZAR**; you can switch to USD). This is
   used for billing, placement fees, and reports throughout the app.
4. Click **Create Account**.

> [SCREENSHOT: The sign-up form with "Company / Recruiter" selected, showing the Company
> Name field and the ZAR/USD currency toggle.]

**What to expect:**
- You may be asked to **confirm your email** before you can sign in — if so, check your
  inbox for a confirmation link. (Whether this step appears depends on your account
  configuration.)
- Once you're in, you land on your **Agency Dashboard** as the account **Admin**.
- Your **7-day free trial starts automatically** — you'll see a countdown banner on the
  dashboard.

---

## Step 2 — (Optional) Add your branding

If you plan to send branded candidate CVs to clients, set your branding first so your logo
appears on them.

1. Open your **Profile** and go to the **Branding** section.
2. Upload your agency logo and save.

> [SCREENSHOT: The Profile → Branding section with a logo upload area.]

> **Good to know:** On the onboarding checklist, the "branding" step is one you tick off
> yourself — the app doesn't automatically detect that you've uploaded a logo.

---

## Step 3 — Screen your first CV

This is the core feature. Go to **CV Screening** (the "Add Candidate via Smart Parser"
quick action on your dashboard also takes you here).

1. **Upload a resume** — drag and drop, or browse for a PDF, DOC, or DOCX file (up to 10MB).
2. Wait a few seconds while the AI reads it. You'll see a **"Resume Parsed & Analyzed!"**
   confirmation.
3. Review the **Extracted Profile** (name, contact, title, a written summary, and skills)
   and the **Core Metrics** card (notice period, salary expectation, hardware).
4. Click **"Save to Talent Pool"** to keep this candidate in your database.

> [SCREENSHOT: The CV Screening page after parsing — the "Extracted Profile" and "Core
> Metrics" cards side by side, with the "Save to Talent Pool" button highlighted.]

> **Heads-up (trial limit):** Your free trial includes **2 CV screenings**. Use them
> deliberately.

> **Known limitation — please read:** When you save a candidate here, the basic details
> (name, email, title, skills) are stored, but some of the richer extracted details (years
> of experience, education, certifications, and the AI summary) **do not yet save to the
> candidate's profile.** Right after saving, the candidate's "Profile Intelligence" section
> may say *"No enriched profile data available yet."* This is a known gap we're working on —
> it's not something you did wrong.

---

## Step 4 — Run a candidate–job match

On the **same CV Screening page**, below the CV upload, you can score the candidate against
a specific job.

1. **Paste the job description** into the job-spec box (or upload it as a file).
2. Optionally turn on **Skills-First Mode** — this weights demonstrated skills and
   real-world experience more heavily than formal degrees. Use it when you care about what
   someone can do more than where they studied.
3. Click **"Start AI Matching."**
4. Review the **AI Match Assessment**: an overall **match score (0–100%)**, a summary,
   matched skills, missing skills, strengths, and notes on experience fit.

> [SCREENSHOT: The "AI Match Assessment" card showing the circular score gauge, matched
> skills (green) and missing skills (amber).]

> **Heads-up (trial limit):** Your free trial includes **3 job matches**.

> **Important — the match score is shown once, on screen:** This score is **not saved** to
> the candidate's record. It will **not** appear later on your candidates list or in the
> dashboard's "Avg AI Score." If you want to keep the result, note it down or capture it now
> while it's on screen. (This is a current limitation — scoring and the candidate database
> aren't yet connected.)

---

## Step 5 — Post a vacancy

1. Go to **Create Vacancy** (also reachable from the "AI Brief Builder" quick action).
2. Fill in the **Vacancy Title** (required), **Description**, **Location**, **Salary**, and
   optionally link a **Client** from the dropdown.
3. *(Optional, Chrome/Edge only)* Click the **microphone** button to dictate the brief out
   loud — the AI will transcribe it and fill in the fields for you to review. Nothing is
   saved until you click save.
4. Click **Save**. You'll see a **"Vacancy created"** confirmation.

> [SCREENSHOT: The "Create Vacancy Brief" form, with the microphone/voice button visible in
> the header and the Client dropdown open.]

> **Where did it go?** After saving, the form clears and you stay on the same page — you are
> **not** taken to the new vacancy. Find it anytime under **Vacancies**.

> **Note:** Creating a vacancy does **not** automatically score or match any candidates
> against it. Matching is a separate, manual step (see Step 4).

---

## Step 6 — Add a client

1. Go to **Clients** and click **"Add Client."**
2. Enter the **Client name** (required), and optionally a contact name, contact email, and
   status (Prospect, Active, On Hold, Inactive).
3. Save. To link a client to a job, use the **Client** dropdown when creating or editing a
   vacancy (Step 5).

> [SCREENSHOT: The "Add Client" dialog with the name and status fields.]

---

## Step 7 — Build a pipeline (optional, once you have candidates and vacancies)

1. Open a candidate from your **Talent Pool**.
2. Use **"Submit to Vacancy"** to add them to a job's pipeline.
3. On the **vacancy page**, move candidates through stages (submitted → reviewing →
   interview → offer → placed, etc.) using the status dropdown.

## What happens next — automated vs. manual steps

This is the most important section for setting your expectations, because **RecruitedAI does
not do anything in the background.** There's nothing to wait for.

- **Nothing runs on its own.** The AI acts *only* when you click a button — the moment you
  upload a CV, click "Start AI Matching," or dictate a job brief. Results appear right then,
  on screen. If you don't see a result, it's because that action hasn't been triggered yet,
  not because something is still processing.
- **There are no emails or notifications.** The app does not send you (or candidates, or
  clients) any emails, and there is no in-app notification bell or alert feed. You won't get
  a "your match is ready" message — matches are ready the instant they finish on screen.
  *(The one possible exception is the sign-up email-confirmation link, which is sent when
  you register.)*
- **Matching does not happen automatically when you post a job.** Posting a vacancy just
  saves it. To score a candidate against it, run a match yourself (Step 4).
- **Your free trial (7 days) includes:** 2 CV screenings, 3 job matches, 1 cover letter,
  1 candidate profile, 1 interview analysis, 2 lead searches, and 3 branded CV exports —
  counted per month. When you reach a limit, you'll see a **"trial limit reached"** message
  for that feature.
- **If an AI step shows "AI temporarily unavailable,"** the AI service was briefly
  unreachable. Your uploaded file is kept — just click **"Try Again."**
- **Shared resumes are private by default.** A resume or online profile is **not** publicly
  viewable until you switch on the **"Public share link"** toggle on the Master Resume or
  Online Resume page. Before you enable it, the `/resume/…` link shows "not found"; switching
  the toggle off again disables the link straight away. Contact details (email, phone,
  LinkedIn) are never shown on the public page — only when you turn sharing on does the link
  work, and even then contact info stays private.

## Known rough edges (so nothing surprises you)

RecruitedAI is in active development. A few things you may bump into early:

- **Match scores aren't saved to candidates** (Step 4) — note them down while on screen.
- **Some parsed CV details don't persist yet** (Step 3) — the enriched profile can look empty
  right after saving.
- **Upgrading isn't self-serve yet.** The "Upgrade" button and billing page **do not process
  real payments today** — clicking through will look like it succeeded, but no plan change
  happens. To move to a paid plan once your trial ends, **contact the team** (the billing
  page shows the contact email). If your trial limits are blocking you, that's the route.
- **Voice dictation is Chrome/Edge only** and needs microphone permission.
- **The "View/Upload Resume" button on a candidate's profile currently does nothing** — it's
  a placeholder.
- **A few pages show "Coming Soon"** (e.g. client activity log, some resume/bio tools) — these
  are visible but not yet functional.

For a fuller list of current limitations, see **`docs/faq-known-issues.md`**.
